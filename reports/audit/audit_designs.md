# Audit of article designs 1–4 (independent fact-check)

Audited 2026-11-05. Files: `reports/design/article_1.md` to `article_4.md`. The designs and the plan doc were not edited.

**Method.**
- Every author-year citation was matched by script against all `reports/sections/*_refs.md` files, and suspect matches were checked by hand.
- Works missing from the refs files were checked against Crossref and Open Library.
- Factual claims were checked against the section texts, `wf_factcheck_key_claims.md` and outside sources.
- TSCS claims were checked against the TSCS questionnaires and project reports downloaded from `www2.ios.sinica.edu.tw/sc/cht/` (files `download-tscs042/092/142/181/232.pdf` and the reports `download-tscs18.pdf` and `download-tscs23.pdf`).
- Journal pages were fetched directly where reachable.

**Severity.** H = factual error or misattribution that would mislead. M = overstatement, construct or arithmetic problem, or internal inconsistency that matters for submission. L = minor wording, citation-form or housekeeping issue. R = a `[verify]` tag that can now be resolved.

### Citation cross-check summary
- Every author-year citation in the four designs was matched against the refs files. All resolve to a refs entry except the items in #26 and #27.
- The designs themselves mark #26 ("novelty notes only").
- Manual review confirmed that hits matched the intended work. - No invented work was found.
