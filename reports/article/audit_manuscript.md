Audit date: 2026-11-11. Target: `reports/article/manuscript_sce.md` (with `title_page_sce.md`, `works_cited_check.md`, `quotation_checklist.md`). The manuscript was not edited.

**How this audit was done.** Every primary text was downloaded again from its public source and searched directly; the reading notes were not relied on. Sources used: Internet Archive `_djvu.txt` files, Project Gutenberg, bible.oremus.org (NRSV), pct.org.tw, china.usc.edu, tcnn.org.tw, vatican.va, the two TSCS report PDFs at www2.ios.sinica.edu.tw, Crossref, OpenAlex, Open Library, and the author-hosted PDF of Ho 2019.

**Limit of this audit.** Scanned books were checked against their OCR text, not against page images. Long-s spellings and line-break hyphens were normalised by eye. Page numbers come from running heads in the OCR. The author still has to look at the page images for the Latin.

**House style.**

- 'et al.' is used for five and four editors, which follows the rule.
