# Studies in Christian Ethics (Sage) — submission requirements

Source: Sage submission-guidelines page (mirror) https://sage.cnpereading.com/storage/ext/submission-guidelines/page/author-instructions_SCE.html, read 2026-11-11. The official page (journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/SCE) returned 403; re-check there before submitting.

- Access: subscription. **No fees to submit or publish.** Optional open access via Sage Choice / institutional agreements.
- Peer review: **double-anonymised**; two independent reviews; desk rejection possible; reviewers chosen by the editor.
- Submit via Sage Track (ScholarOne): https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/sce
- Files: fully anonymised main manuscript + separate Title Page with author details.
- Abstract: unstructured, **150–200 words**. Keywords: **4–6**.
- Word limit: **not stated** on the page (checklist asks for word count). Recent SCE articles should be checked for typical length. [verify]
- Style: **footnotes, house style** (not author-date). Examples:
  - Article: Barrett, 'Theology as Grammar: Regulative Principles or Paradigms and Practices?', *Modern Theology* 25.2 (1988), pp. 155-72.
  - Book: Colin E. Gunton, *The One, The Three and The Many* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2nd edn, 1993), pp. 56-59.
  - Chapter: J.L. Martyn, 'Have we Found Elijah?', in R. Hamerton-Kelly and R. Scroggs (eds.), *Jews, Greeks and Christians* …
  - Repeat citations: short title, e.g. Martyn, 'Have we Found Elijah?', p. 235.
  - Page ranges: pp. 153-79 but pp. 107-109. Avoid f. / ff. 'et al.' for more than three authors.
- Spelling: **-ise** (recognise, emphasise, organisation, analyse); Oxford Dictionary for Writers and Editors. **Single quotation marks**; quotations over four lines indented without quotation marks. Foreign words italicised. Word file preferred.
- Title page must include, under 'Statements and Declarations' (each stated even if not applicable): Declaration of conflicting interest; Funding statement; Ethical considerations; Consent to participate; Consent for publication; Data availability statement; Acknowledgments; ORCID iD of submitting author.
- AI: follow Sage's AI policy (disclose AI use; AI cannot be an author). See research_notes/…/journal_ai_policies.md (Sage: assistance must be disclosed in methods or acknowledgements).
- Time to decision: not stated.
