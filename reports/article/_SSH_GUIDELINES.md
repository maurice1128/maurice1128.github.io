# Social Science History (Cambridge UP, for the Social Science History Association) — submission requirements

Read on cambridge.org 2026-11-11 (preparing-your-materials, submitting-your-materials, about-this-journal, fees-and-pricing, review-process). The "preparing" page was partly truncated; re-check before submission.

- Scope: 'peer-reviewed original articles that combine empirical research with theoretical work, undertake comparisons across time and distance, or contribute to the development of quantitative or qualitative methods of analysis.'
- Length: main text 'no longer than 12,000 words (inclusive of footnotes and references)'. Word count on the title page. Title ≤140 characters incl. spaces; section headings ≤70.
- Abstract ≤250 words. Biographical note ≤600 characters (separate file).
- Style: author-date; Chicago Manual of Style 18th ed. where the house guide is silent. In text: 'Wert (1984: 115–17)'; '(Wilson 1963, 1974)'; '(Cobb et al. 1982)'. Footnotes allowed for material not conveniently in the text. Acknowledgments in the first, unnumbered note. Reference list required.
- Spelling: Merriam-Webster's Collegiate, 11th ed. (American: judgment, focused, traveling). 'e.g.', 'i.e.' roman. Gender-neutral pronouns. States spelled out.
- Review: double-anonymized. Manuscript anonymous; self-citations in the third person.
- Tables: composed in Word, submitted separately, with callouts ('Table 1 about here'). Figures: EPS/TIFF ≥300 dpi, separate files, no colour, callouts. File format .doc or .rtf, 12-point, double-spaced, one-inch margins.
- Supplementary material: optional, online only, not copyedited. Data: authors 'encouraged' to deposit in a repository and cite.
- Submission: Editorial Manager https://www.editorialmanager.com/ssha/default.aspx
- Fees: 'There is no charge for publishing articles in Social Science History under a standard subscription license.' Optional Gold OA APC £2,610 / US$3,655 (institutional agreements may cover).
- AI: AI tools cannot be authors. Use of AI to generate text or images (incl. translation) or to analyse data must be declared: AI-generated text in acknowledgments or a footnote; AI used for data collection or analysis in the methods section; with tool name and version, dates of use, access, and a full description. Basic spelling/formatting checks need no declaration.
- Competing interests declaration required on the title page ('Competing interests: The author(s) declare none').
- Review times: not stated.
