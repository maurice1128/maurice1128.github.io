# Quotation checklist for `manuscript_sce.md`

For the author to check before submission. Every direct quotation in the manuscript is listed in order of appearance, with the edition, the locator and the open-access copy that the reading notes used (`research_notes/Collective suffering and Christian faith/reading/`).

How the quotations were checked so far: each string below was compared by script with the reading notes (and, for the Bible and the PCT documents, with `reports/sections/16_biblical.md`, `16_contemporary.md` and `audit_03_07_16.md`). All match, except the five Latin strings marked **normalised**, where the notes hold the raw OCR of Wilkins's long-s print (`ftipendio`, `forfitan`, `fufficere`, `fuo proprio facerdoti`, `exceffiva`) and the manuscript prints the normalised spelling. The reading notes were made from OCR text files, not from page images, so each quotation still needs to be checked against the page image.

Totals: 42 quotations from historical and modern sources (16 Latin, counting one incipit; 1 Chinese; 25 English) and 23 from the NRSV: 65 in all.
