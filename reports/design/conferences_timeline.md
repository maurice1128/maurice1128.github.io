### (b) 24-month timeline, November 2026 – November 2028

The workloads come from the designs. Article 1: about 280–330 hours (about 15 hours a week for 20–22 weeks). Article 2: about 750–800 hours (about 20 hours a week for 36–40 weeks). Article 3: 9–12 months part-time, with archival access the critical path. Article 4: about 300–410 hours over 7–9 months, plus 150–200 hours if the optional interviews go ahead.

Together that is roughly 1,900–2,300 hours, or 19–23 focused hours a week for the whole period. Article 3 is therefore the buffer: if the weekly hours fall short, its submission moves into 2029. Article 4's interviews are deferred to a later Chinese-language paper. Journal decision times are not known, so the revision windows below are placeholders, used once a decision arrives.

### (c) Checklist before each submission

- [ ] **AI-use disclosure, per journal.** Read the live policy on the day of submission. Confirmed so far: CSSH, *Social Science History* and *Church History* require a declaration (tool, version, dates, use), and AI cannot be an author. *Past & Present* bans AI-generated text or argument, so do not submit there. No AI policy was found on the *Sociology of Religion* or *IBMR* pages. Wiley (*History Compass*, *Religion Compass*, *JRH*, *JSSR*) and the Chinese journals are unconfirmed. Keep a dated log of how AI was used (search, reading notes, fact-checks) so the statement is accurate.
- [ ] **Every direct quotation checked by the author against the source.** Use the page or folio in the reading notes, the original language (Latin, Chinese, POJ) and the edition actually cited (e.g. Wilkins 1737 for Ralph's mandate, not Holmes's calendar).
- [ ] **Every citation verified.** Author, year, title, pages and DOI. Apply the fact-check corrections exactly (Belloc et al.; Robinson 2019 in *Archives* 54/2; Dittmar and Meisenzahl 11–14 points, 14 baseline; the Jedwab aggregate caveat; Madsen's argument; PCT growth in 1955–65). Clear every *[verify]* tag and the HANDOFF "尚未查證" list. Cite works known only at second hand as such.
- [ ] **Anonymisation.** Remove the name, NYCU affiliation, acknowledgements, grant numbers and file metadata. Cite the author's other articles in the series in the third person, or as "Author, under review". Check that tracked changes and comments are gone. The Taiwan-based advantages for Article 3 go in the cover letter, not the text.
- [ ] **Word count including notes**, by the journal's own rule. CSSH: ≤14,000 including abstract, notes and bibliography. SSH: ≤12,000 including footnotes and references. *JRH*: about 8,000 including footnotes *[verify]*. *History Compass*: about 5,000 *[verify with the editor]*. *JSSR*: ≤10,000 including references *[verify]*. *Sociology of Religion*: ≤35 pages, abstract ≤150 words. *IBMR*: 4,000–7,000 including endnotes. 《新史學》 研究討論: about 10,000 字 including notes and bibliography.
- [ ] **Style.** Chicago author-date for the series drafts; convert where required. *IBMR* takes Chicago 17th, endnotes only. *Church History* uses house style with endnotes. *Sociology of Religion* uses ASA plus Chicago author-date. SSH uses its house style, then Chicago 18th. Check the abstract length, keywords, heading levels and romanisation (pinyin or Wade-Giles; POJ for Taiwanese) for consistency.
- [ ] **Permissions for images.** Cover manuscript images and archive photographs (PCT Archives, *Taiwan Church News*, Maryknoll), maps and WPA or LOC images. Record the rights holder, licence and credit line for each. Get written permission before submission where the journal requires it.
- [ ] **Data availability for Article 4.** Write a statement that points to SRDA, GESIS and the WVS for the microdata, which cannot be redistributed if the SRDA user agreement says so *[verify terms]*. Deposit the R/Stata code, the OSF pre-registration link and the codebook-harmonisation table. Report weights and missing-data handling. Add the ethics statement (exempt secondary data, or approval number).
- [ ] **No duplicate publication.** Chinese companions must be new texts on different questions, cite the English article and be disclosed to both editors. Check each journal's prior-publication and preprint policy. Check whether conference full papers (for example the 臺灣社會學會 upload) are posted publicly. Never have one manuscript under review at two journals.
- [ ] **Cover letter.** Cover fit with the journal (name a precedent article), the contribution in two sentences, the word count by the journal's rule, the AI declaration, related series papers under review, any conflicts of interest and, for CSSH, the APC or waiver request. For Article 3, add the archival access the author has.
- [ ] **Suggested and opposed reviewers**, if the journal asks. Suggest three to five people with no co-authorship, supervision or institutional tie, spread across history, sociology and theology. Do not suggest anyone thanked in the acknowledgements. Name opposed reviewers only with a reason.
