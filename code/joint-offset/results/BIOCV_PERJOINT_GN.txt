=== (3) GN ESTIMATOR AUDIT ===
rowv.triangulate_wdlt scales DLT ROWS by w  -> the SVD minimises sum w^2 r^2.
lm_tri accumulates JtJ += w*(J^T J)         -> GN minimises sum w r^2.
So the published 'GN vs DLT' contrast compares two different objectives.

READ: if DLT-vs-GN shrinks or reverses once the objective is matched, Section 3.1's
heteroscedasticity interpretation is unsupported and the published contrast is an
artefact of the w vs w^2 mismatch, not a finding about estimators.
