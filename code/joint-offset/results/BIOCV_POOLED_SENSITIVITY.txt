CAVEAT ON POOLING. The declared families test different questions on partly overlapping
observations: the per-joint family excludes the pooled [pos:HKA] rows precisely because
those belong to the main family, and the joint-set interactions are computed from the same
per-participant means as the main family rather than from an independent pass. Pooling
therefore double-counts information and is conservative in a way that is not principled,
which is why the manuscript does not adopt it. It is reported because a reader is entitled
to see what the stricter convention would do.
