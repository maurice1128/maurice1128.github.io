NOTE ON SIGN: the adaptive criterion and the criterion at matched k=2 act on the knee in
OPPOSITE directions -- adaptive improves it, matched-k degrades it, both at small p. The
mechanism account is therefore specific to the retention rule and the manuscript must say
so wherever it states the mechanism.
