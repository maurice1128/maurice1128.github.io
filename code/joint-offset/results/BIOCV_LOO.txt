WHY A POPULATION TABLE HARMS THE ANGLE: does the harm track the angle change the
own-versus-population offset mismatch predicts? Positive harm = the table RAISES the error.
The predictor is the angle change obtained by applying the mismatch to the reference points,
which is what a three-point angle actually responds to; a common translation cancels.
