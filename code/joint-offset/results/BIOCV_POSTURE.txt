READ: if the offset components swing significantly between the lowest and highest
flexion quintiles, the 'fixed per-participant-per-joint offset' of Section 3.5 is
posture-dependent. Consequences if so: part of the decomposition's 'random' component
is structured, a static correction table cannot work even in principle (which is
consistent with the LOO correction worsening knee flexion by 0.606 deg), and the knee's
flexion-sensitive offset component is itself a function of the angle being measured --
a circularity the manuscript must disclose. If the swings are small or non-significant,
the fixed-offset account stands and the LOO failure is due to across-participant
magnitude variation alone, as Section 3.5 currently argues.
