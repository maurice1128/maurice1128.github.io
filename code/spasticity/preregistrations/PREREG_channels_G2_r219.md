## 2. ⛔ THE BAND QUESTION — which criterion, and why

**The r209 control band was pre-registered for the 13.58-second window.**

⛔ **Testing G2-window measurements against the 13.58-window band numbers is invalid: the two are
measured over different intervals and are not the same quantity.**
⛔ **Inventing a band after seeing the G2 numbers is worse than invalid.**

⭐ **REGISTERED CHOICE: the band is RECOMPUTED under G2 by the SAME PRE-REGISTERED RULE — min and max
over the six control-arm seeds, per channel.** *The **rule** predates the data; only the window changes.
Applying a pre-registered rule to a different interval is not a new criterion. **The control arm is the
reference arm, not a comparison arm, so recomputing it does not use the contrast under test.***

⚠ **The 13.58 band is also reported, for transparency only. It is NOT the criterion and no verdict
rests on it.**
