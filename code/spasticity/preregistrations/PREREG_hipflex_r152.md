## 0. ⚠ THE ORIGIN OF THIS HYPOTHESIS, STATED FIRST BECAUSE IT LIMITS EVERYTHING BELOW

**This channel was NOT predicted. It was found in the re-optimised 3D study (r151) as the ONE endpoint
of twelve on which the spastic arm is displaced from an uninjected control:**

| | C | S | W |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hip_flexion_r` range | 55.050–55.561 | **57.482–58.513** | 55.933–56.222 |

⛔ **A hypothesis generated by one dataset and tested on another is legitimate — and it is POST HOC. It
is not the registered primary of any study, and no result from it may ever be reported as one.**

⭐ **The reason it is worth testing: the channel is SAGITTAL.** *`hip_flexion_r` exists in `H0914M`. The
out-of-plane hypothesis that §6 of `PREREG_3d_reopt_r151.md` registered as the entire reason for doing
3D is NOT the one that delivered — a planar model could have measured this. **So the question is whether
the 2D study missed a real signal by choosing the wrong joint.***

## 1. The question

> **In the 2D corpus, does contralateral hip flexion range separate the spastic conditions from the
> weak ones — and does it survive the adjustment that killed `ank_vel_max`?**

**The lesion is left-sided throughout this project** *(the registered analysis reads `side="l"`;
`legs = left`)*. ⛔ **"Contralateral" therefore means `hip_flexion_r`, and that is fixed here.**

## 2. Corpus, and why NOT the slope corpus

**Corpus: `scone/replay_crossed/` — 12 conditions × 4 seeds, already replayed, the corpus on which
`ank_vel_max` produced the reference numbers.**

| | spastic | weak |
|---|---|---|
| conditions | `DR2K050`, `DR2K075`, `DR2K100`, `DR2K150`, `DR2K200` | `PAR20`, `PAR40`, `PAR60`, `CMW70`, `CMW80` |

⛔ **The 234-run slope corpus is REJECTED for this test, and the reason is arithmetic.** *At D = 0 it
offers two spastic conditions and two weak ones. **2 v 2 gives a minimum attainable two-sided p of
2/C(4,2) = 0.3333** — the dead floor `PREREG_shape_r138.md` §6 already established. Seed-level testing
there would give large n but the seeds descend from **two** lesions, which is pseudo-replication.*

⭐ **`replay_crossed` gives 5 v 5 at CONDITION level, minimum p = 2/C(10,5) = 0.007937, and it is the
same design, same unit and same corpus as the registered residual test — so the result is directly
comparable to `ank_vel_max`'s 0.3600 / 0.5476 / 4-of-5 / 0.8030.**

## 3. Metric

**Mean per-cycle hip flexion RANGE, in degrees, computed exactly as `sto_utils.cycle_features`
computes it** — *GRF-delimited cycles on the same side, `settle = 1.0 s`, last cycle dropped,
`hip_flexion_<side>` converted from radians.* ⛔ **NOT global range.** *Condition value = mean over that
condition's 4 seeds.*

## 4. The tests, and the pool is fixed at 3

| # | test | role |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **`hip_flexion_r`** raw, spastic vs weak | ⭐ **PRIMARY** — the 3D-derived channel |
| 2 | `hip_flexion_l` raw | ipsilateral control: if BOTH sides separate, this is not a contralateral compensation signal |
| 3 | `hip_flexion_l − hip_flexion_r` raw | the asymmetry form |

**Exact two-sided Mann–Whitney, 5 v 5, AUC = U/25, overlap counted as `residual_test.py` counts it.**
⛔ **Bonferroni ×3. A perfect separation reaches p_adj = 0.0238, which clears 0.05.**

## 5. ⛔ THE RESIDUAL TEST IS THE ONE THAT DECIDES, AND IT IS REGISTERED NOW

**A raw separation that dies under adjustment is the exact shape this project has found four times.**
*`ank_vel_max` was AUC 0.0000 raw and 0.3600 residual; all six r139 shape features died the same way;
r153 showed the confounding is invariant across 13 measurement pipelines.*

**Step 2, registered here so it cannot be an afterthought:** *regress each of the three quantities on
**`ank_rom`, `cycle_time`, `ank_hs`** — the identical covariates and the identical procedure as
`scone/residual_test.py` — and re-test the residual by the same exact Mann–Whitney.*

| outcome | what it means |
|---|---|
| **raw separates AND residual separates** | ⭐ **the only outcome that would be a finding** — a signal not reducible to sagittal ROM, cadence and equinus, in a channel the 2D study never tested |
| **raw separates, residual does not** | ⛔ **the fifth instance of the same shape. The 2D study did NOT miss a usable signal; it chose a joint whose separation is the same confound.** |
| **raw does not separate** | **the 3D `hip_flexion_r` displacement does not reproduce in 2D at all** — a fact about the 3D benchmark, not about the lesions |

## 6. ⚠ REGISTERED IN ADVANCE: even a full positive is weak

**Contralateral hip flexion is a COMPENSATION, not a lesion sign.** *An intact limb flexing more
because the other ankle is stiff is exactly the confound `PREREG_shape_r138.md` §3 registered against
Route C: it is consistent with "asymmetry tracks impairment severity" and does **not** distinguish
"this is spasticity" from "this side is impaired somehow".*

⛔ **So a positive here would license: "a sagittal channel the 2D study did not measure separates these
conditions." It would NOT license: "2D video can tell spasticity from weakness."** *The clinical claim
needs a signal that identifies the LESION TYPE, and a compensation magnitude does not.*

⚠ **n = 5 conditions per arm, 4 seeds each. Seeds are optimiser restarts, not subjects. No
sensitivity, specificity or patient-level AUC may be quoted.**
