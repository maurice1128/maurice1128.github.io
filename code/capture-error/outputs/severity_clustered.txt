--- the same two contrasts as a t interval on the five per-body losses ---
At five clusters a percentile bootstrap is confined to the convex hull of the five body
means, so when they agree in sign it cannot cross zero whatever the true uncertainty
(cluster_degeneracy.py). The t interval can fail, so it is the one the paper reports.

READING: the claim is the conjunction "the moderate case loses more than the mild case AND
more than the severe case". As an intersection-union test it needs both components to
reject and needs no multiplicity widening. Under the strict unit that conjunction is
  under the percentile bootstrap : supported
  under the t interval           : NOT SUPPORTED

The t interval is the one to report: it is the estimator used for Table 4, and it is the
one that can fail at five clusters. The claim is rescoped to the observation - the moderate
case shows the largest loss, in four bodies of five, without a separable difference.
