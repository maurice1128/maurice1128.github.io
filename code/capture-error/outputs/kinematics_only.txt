=== WHAT A CAMERA ALONE DELIVERS ===
The reported "camera" tier is 33 features: 18 sagittal kinematics, 9 asymmetry, 9
spatiotemporal minus exclusions. In this model the spatiotemporal features are derived from
vertical ground reaction force, so a camera without a force plate would not have them.
The kinematics-only tier is the 27 kinematic and asymmetry features.

 capture error  33-feature  27-feature  difference
           0 deg       0.845       0.824      -0.021
           2 deg       0.824       0.875      +0.051
           5 deg       0.659       0.742      +0.083
           8 deg       0.600       0.623      +0.023
          12 deg       0.549       0.536      -0.013

  33-feature: 0.845 at 0 deg -> 0.549 at 12 deg, 2.4x the 0.227 baseline at the end
  27-feature: 0.824 at 0 deg -> 0.536 at 12 deg, 2.4x
  mean cost of dropping the force-derived timing features: +0.025

READING: if the 27-feature curve stays well above baseline the tolerance claim holds for a
camera on its own, and the paper should report this curve as its headline. If it collapses,
the reported curve describes an instrumented setup and the claim has to say so.
