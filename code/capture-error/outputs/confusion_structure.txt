=== PERSISTENCE OF THE OFF-DIAGONAL PATTERN ===
Spearman correlation between the off-diagonal cells of two error levels.
The null is the same correlation after independently permuting one matrix.

  comparison             rho        p   null rho (95th pct)
  2 deg vs    5 deg   0.652   0.0019                 0.374
  2 deg vs    8 deg   0.734   0.0002                 0.383
  2 deg vs   12 deg   0.549   0.0122                 0.388

READING: if the observed correlations sit well above the permutation null, the pattern
of mistakes is a stable property of the mechanics rather than an artefact that appears
and disappears with the noise level. That does not remove the circularity objection -
only patient data can - but it does discriminate between the two explanations that
objection conflates.
