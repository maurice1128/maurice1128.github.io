READING: the sentence "the steepest degradation falls inside the 2-5 deg band" is a
statement about point estimates and is true as such. The sentence that follows it - that
the convention therefore LOCATES CORRECTLY where accuracy is most sensitive - is a claim
that the 2-5 deg rate differs from its neighbours, and it needs these intervals. Where
they include zero, that step is not licensed and the observation must stand alone.
