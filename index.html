Mu-Hua (Maurice) Wang

I work on physical assistive robotics for long-term care: robots that carry out contact-rich care tasks on a bedridden human body, starting with diaper changing. That task demands three things at once — perceiving a body that will not cooperate, predicting how it moves when it is touched, and manipulating deformable material safely against skin.

My work so far covers all three. I built and clinically validated an RGB multi-view markerless motion capture system at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, wrote learned dynamics models of musculoskeletal bodies, and measured whether compositional motion priors survive transfer to a robot hand. I hold a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from NYCU and an M.S. place there on approved leave; I am looking for a lab where these pieces can be assembled into a machine that does the task.

Note on how the projects below are written. Each one lists the boundary it stops at next to the result. Every number traces to a stored run artifact, non-significant cells are reported as not detected rather than as absent, and the negative findings are reported as findings.

Two Routes to a Motor Prior: Guidance Versus a Frozen Body Model
Mu-Hua Wang
Submitted, Transactions on Machine Learning Research

A frozen forward model learned from task-agnostic, reward-free motor babbling, against a distilled teacher policy, under one SAC backbone on MyoSuite musculoskeletal bodies. Seven controls and a matched withdrawal at a common training step. Guidance buys the early speed; on myoFinger that lead reverses inside the budget.

Against itself: on myoFinger no condition is shown to beat model-free SAC at the endpoint, the coach included. One matched control was never run, and the paper says so.

Which Hand-Motion Composition Axes Are Worth Measuring
Mu-Hua Wang
Submitted, IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters

Modular motion priors rest on one premise: novel recombinations of the primitives stay usable. It is almost never measured. This builds the measurement — a paired split over six axes of three public hand datasets, behind five gates — plus a raw-data interaction statistic that runs in minutes, before any model is trained.

Against itself: the paper's own negative control removed its strongest result. On a bundle whose compositional penalty is zero by construction, the normalised metric still returns 90 % of the headline figure.

What Master-Then-Reorganise Does Not Buy: An Invariance Argument for Body Codes and Its Two Boundaries
Mu-Hua Wang
Manuscript, 2026

With the dynamics core frozen and only a low-dimensional body code θ adapted, replacing θ by Mθ and the code's weight block by WθM−1 leaves the function unchanged. A large family of "reorganise the code" proposals is settled before any experiment runs. Then the two conditions that identity needs are measured.

Against itself: three of five planned mechanisms ran at two seeds with no stored result file, and their numbers are withdrawn entirely rather than reported weakly.

Both put a clinical decision, not a measurement, on the dependent axis, in forward dynamics where ground truth is known by construction.

How Much Joint-Angle Error Can the Identification of a Weak Muscle Group Tolerate? A Predictive-Simulation Study
Mu-Hua Wang, Hui-Ting Shih
Manuscript in preparation

The field's rule for good-enough motion capture — 2° acceptable, 2–5° needs consideration — scores measurement quality and had never been tested against a decision. Weakness is imposed one muscle group at a time and error added in controlled amounts: top-1 identification falls 0.845 → 0.549 as joint-angle error grows 0° → 12°, over 66 design cells.

Against itself: the largest observed loss is the moderate deficit, not the mild one — but with five model variants that difference is not statistically separable, and the page says so.

Joint-Centre Offset Dominates 3D Position Error and Can Reverse Comparisons Between Markerless Triangulation Methods
Mu-Hua Wang, Hui-Ting Shih
Manuscript in preparation

Markerless pipelines are tuned on 3D position error against a marker-based reference. But pose-estimation keypoints and marker-based joint centres are not the same anatomical construct. Across 104 walking trials from 11 participants on a nine-camera ring, the keypoint lay 27–37 mm from the reference joint centre — 74–81 % of squared position error within a participant, against 2.5 mm for the largest triangulation effect measured.

Against itself: the hip offset cannot be apportioned between the two systems, because the reference hip centre is itself a regression whose published accuracy is the same size as the offset reported. The hip carries the largest reversal, which the paper says it cannot explain.

Distinguishing Graded Plantarflexor Hyperreflexia from Graded Dorsiflexor Weakness in Simulated Post-Stroke Gait: A Search over 81 Sagittal Kinematic Variables
Mu-Hua Wang, Hui-Ting Shih
Manuscript in preparation

Post-stroke equinovarus has two opposite causes calling for opposite treatments, and telling them apart currently needs an invasive diagnostic nerve block. 81 sagittal candidates scanned across three joints and the whole gait cycle, in a 19-DoF, 22-muscle model where both lesion magnitudes are known.

Against itself: the two lesion groups are completely confounded with falling — 23 of 23 hyperreflexia runs fell, 36 of 36 weakness runs finished — and that confound cannot be closed.

— Behavioural data science —

Consumed Methadone Dose Patterns Around Randomly Scheduled Urine Testing
Mu-Hua Wang, Tso-Jeng Wang
Manuscript in preparation

A methods problem disguised as a clinical one: the discriminating feature of a dose trajectory is its shape, but shape is confounded with magnitude and lives on a space Euclidean clustering gets wrong. Mapping the (post, pre) slope pair to a polar angle puts it on a torus, where a bivariate von Mises mixture can find it. 9,900 urine tests from 552 patients, 2018–2022.

Against itself: the dose-change association did not replicate out of time across an April 2020 split, which the paper treats as a qualification of the primary claim rather than a limitation; and the trajectory adds essentially nothing predictive over attendance alone.

Systems

RGB Multi-View 3D Markerless Motion Capture (NTKCAP)
NYCU · research assistant Oct 2023 – Sep 2024, team lead Oct 2024 – Sep 2025 · advisor Prof. Hui-Ting Shih

Real-time whole-body pose sensing for clinical use — the perception layer a robot working over a bed has to have. I wrote the pipeline, ran the validation experiments, and collected the clinical data myself.

  • GPU acceleration and CPU parallelism across capture, inference, triangulation and inverse kinematics; DLT triangulation with occlusion handling; and ROWV, an outlier-rejection algorithm for small-sample settings, faster and more accurate than KNN and RANSAC on this problem.
  • VICON comparison over 21 participants, plus 104 walking trials from 11 participants on a nine-camera ring.
  • Shipped the product, not only the algorithm: clinician-facing PyQt6 GUI, SQL and cloud pipeline with ICD-10 coding, Unreal Engine rehabilitation game.
  • Field deployment: 2 signed hospital MOUs (Taipei Veterans General; Taipei City Hospital), 200+ stroke and knee-pain patients in Vietnam, 40+ stroke patients with exoskeleton at Kaohsiung Chang Gung, 20+ elderly at a Kaohsiung daycare centre, amputee feedback trials with NYCU Physical Therapy. Led a 5-person team; 4 conference presentations, 6 media features.
