What a Motor Prior Is Worth:
A Body Model and a Teacher Compared to Convergence and Through Withdrawal

Take a head start away, and wait until learning has converged: the learner that lost it is never behind one that never had it. What remains is a shortfall, not dependence.

Mu-Hua (Maurice) Wang · National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University

[Paper (PDF)] [Code] [All projects]

This paper merges and supersedes two earlier drafts (Two Routes to a Motor Prior, which scored every condition at a 12,000-step endpoint, and a separate teacher-withdrawal study). Not posted to any preprint server.

Abstract

The guidance hypothesis holds that guidance breeds dependence: the longer a learner is guided, the larger its fall when guidance is withdrawn. We test it, and the same question for a model of the body, in one soft actor-critic learner on six muscle-driven bodies, two from MyoSuite and four planar arms of one to four joints, given a frozen forward model learned from reward-free babbling or a frozen teacher acting through a distillation term, with matched seeds, an adversarial control for each prior and training to a checked plateau. Replacing a withdrawn teacher by a frozen snapshot of the student, which keeps the loss term but adds no information, separates what withdrawal removes: the teacher's share of the immediate drop falls with how long it was attached, the deleted term's does not. Once training has converged, a learner that lost its teacher is never detectably worse than one never guided, at any attachment duration, on either body, whether guidance supplemented or replaced its own gradient; with a competent teacher it is better, by more the longer it was guided. A withdrawn body model can leave the learner short of one that kept it, never behind one that never had it. What remains after withdrawal is a shortfall against keeping the prior, not dependence. What the priors are worth is bound to their content: the babble model beats model-free learning on four of six bodies and a random model on all six, and a teacher is worth its competence.

Taking the head start away

Withdrawal as the literature performs it changes two things at once. Zeroing a teacher's distillation weight deletes a loss term as well as the teacher's information. The control here is a self-anchor: at withdrawal the teacher is replaced by a frozen copy of the student itself, so the loss term survives with the same weight and form but carries nothing further. The literature's own measure of dependence is the withdrawn learner against one never guided.

The same holds when guidance replaces the learner's own gradient (cloning only until withdrawal): at 100k plain SAC after withdrawal is level with a never-guided learner on the elbow and 38–59 mm ahead of it on the finger. The two bodies differ in what the collapse at withdrawal is: on the elbow it is switching on the student's own gradient, and keeping the teacher does not prevent it; on the finger it is the deleted term, and keeping the teacher does.

And for the body model. Withdrawn at 8k with the learner's real-data signal held fixed, it costs the elbow +22.11 [+4.94, +39.28] mrad at 12k, where the standard protocol detects nothing, and is not detected at 100k. On the finger it leaves the learner 15 mm short of one that kept the model and 25 mm ahead of one that never had it.

Scope statements that travel with every number above

  • Single-task reach, in simulation, on deterministic and fully observed bodies; the one-joint bodies' held-out targets interpolate between the training targets.
  • The body-model condition is handed the environment's analytic reward at imagined states. The self-anchor holds whatever the student had absorbed by withdrawal; the convergence claim rests on the withdrawn-against-never-guided contrast, which needs no anchor.
  • The finger's first teacher had drifted to within 3.2 mm of doing nothing. Withdrawal is a single step; contrasts were not pre-registered.

Method

One soft actor-critic backbone in every condition (two hidden layers of 128, batch 128, update-to-data ratio 2), at least 12 matched seeds per condition, and evaluation on twenty held-out targets disjoint from the eight training targets. The body model is a forward model learned once from task-agnostic, reward-free Ornstein–Uhlenbeck babbling, then frozen and used for short-horizon Dyna rollouts. The teacher is attached through a distillation term on the student's actor loss, either adding to the student's own gradient or replacing it until withdrawal.

Each prior has an adversarial control: a random model in the same imagination machinery, a random teacher in the same loss. Withdrawing the body model has a matched-replay condition that holds real data per update fixed; withdrawing the teacher has the self-anchor, a random anchor, and in the cloning-only regime a condition that switches on the student's gradient while keeping the teacher. Every condition runs to 100,000 steps and is checked for a plateau.

What was run, and how it was checked

training runs

Six bodies, about 1,500 single-thread CPU-hours. Every condition has at least twelve matched seeds; the three- and four-joint arms have twenty-four.

The babble data is task-agnostic and reward-free, recorded before any task exists.
